## Target Audience Definition

### Primary Personas

**Persona 1: Equity Research Analyst**
- **Title:** Analyst, Associate, Senior Analyst
- **Company Size:** 10-500 employees (boutique to mid-size funds)
- **Pain Points:**
  - Spend 10-15 hours/week on data gathering and formatting
  - Manual comp table updates
  - Repetitive quarterly analysis
  - Limited time for actual insights
- **Buying Triggers:**
  - Quarterly earnings season (busy, need efficiency)
  - New coverage initiations
  - Team capacity constraints

**Persona 2: Family Office Investment Team**
- **Title:** Investment Analyst, Portfolio Manager, Director of Research
- **Company Size:** 1-20 person teams
- **Pain Points:**
  - Small team, broad coverage universe
  - Need institutional-grade analysis without big budget
  - Ad-hoc requests from principals
  - No Bloomberg/FactSet budget for junior staff
- **Buying Triggers:**
  - Adding new mandate (e.g., tech allocation)
  - Key person risk (one analyst covering too much)
  - Principal asking for specific analysis

**Persona 3: Independent Investor/Advisor**
- **Title:** RIA, Independent Wealth Manager, Solo GP
- **Pain Points:**
  - Wearing multiple hats (analysis + client relations)
  - Can't justify $24k/year Bloomberg subscription
  - Need professional-looking reports for clients
  - Time-starved
- **Buying Triggers:**
  - Client asks tough questions about holdings
  - Preparing for client reviews
  - Launching new strategy

### Negative Personas (Avoid)

❌ Students (no real workflow to optimize)
❌ Retail day traders (not target use case)
❌ Large bank/BB analyst desks (entrenched tools, hard to switch)
❌ Quant funds (need programmatic access, not UI)

## Email Sequence Strategy

### Philosophy

**Core Principles:**
1. **Short & Scannable:** Max 100 words per email
2. **Founder Voice:** Personal "I" not corporate "we"
3. **No Pitch First:** Lead with curiosity/pain point
4. **Low Friction CTA:** "Reply 'yes'" not "Book 30-min demo"
5. **Early Access Frame:** "Help us build" not "Buy our product"
6. **Rapid Follow-ups:** Don't wait 7 days, send next email in 3 days

### Sequence Structure (7-Touch Campaign)

| Email | Day | Goal | Open Rate Target | Reply Rate Target |
|-------|-----|------|------------------|-------------------|
| Email 1 | 0 | Pattern interrupt, curiosity | 50%+ | 2-5% |
| Email 2 | 3 | Value prop, social proof | 40%+ | 3-6% |
| Email 3 | 6 | Address objection | 35%+ | 2-4% |
| Email 4 | 10 | Breakup/last chance | 30%+ | 5-8% |
| Email 5 | 14 | New angle (for non-responders) | 25%+ | 2-3% |
| Email 6 | 21 | Re-engagement (case study) | 20%+ | 1-2% |
| Email 7 | 35 | Final breakup | 15%+ | 3-5% |

**Total Expected Response Rate:** 10-15% (Industry standard: 1-5%)

## LinkedIn Message Strategy

### Should You Use LinkedIn Messages?

**✅ YES, if:**
- Your LinkedIn account is >2 years old (established credibility)
- You have >500 connections (looks organic)
- You're okay with ~50 connection requests/week (safe limit)
- Your profile is polished (professional photo, complete bio)

**❌ NO, if:**
- New LinkedIn account (<6 months old) → High ban risk
- Already doing aggressive outreach → Don't add more risk
- Profile looks sales-y → People won't accept

### LinkedIn vs Email: Key Differences

| Factor | Email | LinkedIn |
|--------|-------|----------|
| **Open Rate** | 40-50% | 60-80% (if connection accepted) |
| **Response Rate** | 3-5% | 8-15% (warmer channel) |
| **Spam Risk** | Medium (CAN-SPAM protects) | High (account ban risk) |
| **Character Limit** | Unlimited | 300 chars (connection request) |
| **Best For** | Scale (1000s/month) | High-value targets (100s/month) |
| **Cost** | Low ($0.50/lead) | High (time-intensive) |

**Recommendation:** Use LinkedIn as **secondary channel** for high-value targets who don't respond to email.

### LinkedIn Safety Best Practices

**Do's:**
✅ Send 20-30 connection requests/day max
✅ Personalize every request (use {{firstName}}, {{companyName}})
✅ Mix with organic activity (like posts, comment on content)
✅ Wait 24-48hrs between connection + first message
✅ Use LinkedIn during business hours (9am-5pm local time)
✅ Accept incoming connections (look like a real user)

**Don'ts:**
❌ Send >50 requests/day (instant red flag)
❌ Copy-paste identical messages (spam detection)
❌ Send messages to non-connections (requires InMail credits)
❌ Use new LinkedIn account for outreach (<6 months old)
❌ Only send requests, never engage with content (bot signal)
❌ Send connection requests at 3am (bot signal)

**If You Get Restricted:**
- LinkedIn will show "Weekly invitation limit reached"
- Restriction lasts 1-4 weeks
- Stop ALL activity immediately
- Resume slowly (10 requests/day) after restriction lifts
- Consider this a warning—next ban is permanent

### Decision Framework: Email-Only vs Multi-Channel

**Use Email-Only if:**
- You have 1000+ leads to contact (need scale)
- Leads are mid-level (Analyst, Associate)
- Budget-conscious (save $29/month)
- Risk-averse (protect LinkedIn account)

**Add LinkedIn if:**
- Targeting <200 high-value leads (VP+, decision makers)
- Email response rate <3% after 2 weeks (need new channel)
- Selling high-ticket ($10k+ ACV) product (worth extra effort)
- Have established LinkedIn presence (>500 connections)

**For Fundley (MVP Stage):**

**My Recommendation: Start Email-Only**

**Why:**
- You need volume (500-1000 leads) to find PMF
- Email is safer (no ban risk)
- Email scales better (10x more sends/month)
- Early adopters care about product, not channel
- Save LinkedIn for when you have proven messaging

**When to Add LinkedIn:**
- Month 2+: After you've tested email messaging
- For dream accounts only (top 50 target companies)
- When email response rate stabilizes (know what works)

## Follow-up Cadence

### Response Handling Playbook

#### Scenario 1: Positive Reply ("Yes, interested")

**Immediate Action (within 1 hour):**
```
Amazing! Sending you access now.

Login: [Link]
Quick start guide: [2-min video]

Try this first query: "Analyze [their sector] leader's revenue growth"

I'll check in Friday to see how it went. Questions before then? Just reply.

```

**Follow-up Timeline:**
- Day 3: "How's it going?" check-in
- Day 7: "Quick feedback?" request
- Day 14: "Still using it?" re-engagement
- Day 30: "What's next?" roadmap discussion

#### Scenario 2: "Not Right Now" Reply

**Immediate Action:**
```
Totally understand – wrong timing is a thing.

Mind if I ask: what would make it the *right* time?
- Different features?
- Different stage in quarter?
- Need approval first?

Either way, I'll follow up in [timeframe they mention] if that works?

– [Your Name]
```

**Create Reminder:** Follow up in specified timeframe (default: 3 months)

#### Scenario 3: "Tell Me More" Reply

**Immediate Action:**
```
Happy to! Two options:

1. **Quick version:** 2-min Loom demo → [Link]
2. **Live version:** 10-min screenshare → [Calendly link]

What works better for you?

– [Your Name]

P.S. Most folks prefer option 1 first, then book option 2 if interested.
```

#### Scenario 4: "Not Interested" Reply

**Immediate Action:**
```
No worries at all – thanks for letting me know!

One quick question (helps me improve outreach):

Was it:
- Not a fit for your workflow?
- Already have something similar?
- Timing issue?
- Something else?

Appreciate the honesty either way.

– [Your Name]
```

**Action:** Add to "Do Not Contact" list, analyze feedback

#### Scenario 5: Harsh/Negative Reply

**Immediate Action:**
```
Really sorry for the unwanted email – removing you now.

Appreciate you taking the time to reply (even if it was to tell me to stop).

All the best with your work.

– [Your Name]
```

**Action:**
- Immediate unsubscribe
- Review: Was email poorly targeted? Update ICP.
- Don't take it personally (happens to everyone)

**Track Weekly:**

| Metric | Target | Calculation | Action if Below Target |
|--------|--------|-------------|----------------------|

| **Click Rate** | >15% | Clicks / Opens | Improve CTA placement |
| **Reply Rate** | >8% | Replies / Delivered | Test email body copy |
| **Positive Reply Rate** | >3% | Interested / Delivered | Refine targeting/ICP |
| **Signup Conversion** | >60% | Signups / Positive Replies | Improve onboarding |
| **Active User Rate** | >40% | Active Users / Signups | Fix product onboarding |

### Leading Indicators (Weekly Check)

**Week 1-2:**
- Open rate >40%? ✅ Subject lines working
- Reply rate >5%? ✅ Email content resonating
- Bounce rate <5%? ✅ List quality good

**Week 3-4:**
- Conversion rate >50%? ✅ Onboarding smooth
- Active user rate >30%? ✅ Product has PMF signal

**Month 2:**
- Word-of-mouth signups? ✅ Product-market fit emerging
- Unsolicited feature requests? ✅ Users are engaged
- Users running >10 analyses/week? ✅ Real workflow adoption

### Red Flags (Abort/Pivot Signals)

🚨 **Stop Campaign if:**
- Spam complaint rate >0.1% (>1 per 1,000 emails)
- Bounce rate >10% (list is bad)
- Reply rate <2% after 500 sends (messaging broken)
- Positive replies but 0 signups (onboarding broken)
- Signups but 0 active users (product broken)

Fundley, Inc. | [Physical Address]

## FAQ

**Q: What if I get marked as spam?**
A: Stop immediately. Review targeting (wrong audience?). Warm up email domain. Reduce daily send volume.

**Q: What's a good response rate?**
A: 5-10% is excellent for cold email. <2% means messaging is broken.

**Q: Should I buy lead lists?**
A: No. Bought lists have bad emails, hurt deliverability, and violate GDPR.

**Q: How many emails before I give up?**
A: 7 touchpoints over 35 days. After that, move on.

**Q: Can I automate LinkedIn outreach too?**
A: Risky (against LinkedIn ToS). If you do, use Phantombuster carefully (max 50 connects/day).
