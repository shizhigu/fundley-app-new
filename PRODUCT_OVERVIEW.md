# Fundley Landing Page Blueprint

This document outlines the content, layout, and visual direction for Fundley’s marketing landing page. It is written for designers, marketers, and copywriters who need a single source of truth to build a high-impact, user-facing site.

## Audience & Promise
- **Primary audience:** Investment professionals across private equity, asset management, hedge funds, corporate strategy teams, and financial consultants seeking faster analysis cycles.
- **Core promise:** “Fundley is the AI co-pilot that turns every investment question into decision-ready insight—without manual data wrangling.”
- **Proof themes:** Multi-agent orchestration, trusted outputs, dramatically shorter research cycles, and transparent workflows tailored for institutional teams.

## Page Goals
1. Communicate how Fundley reshapes financial analysis from question to delivery.
2. Showcase concrete product capabilities, differentiators, and scenarios.
3. Drive demo requests and PoC conversations via clear calls-to-action.

## Page Structure & Copy Guidance
### 1. Hero Section (Above the Fold)
- **Headline:** “Turn complex investment questions into decision-ready answers.”
- **Subhead:** “Fundley’s AI analyst team combines financial expertise, narrative insight, and real-time collaboration in one workspace built for capital markets.”
- **Primary CTA:** `Book a Live Demo` (filled button). Secondary link: `Explore Product Walkthrough`.
- **Visual:** Full-width mockup or animated sequence of the product cockpit (chat timeline, data panels). Overlay subtle glowing gradient to echo the AI co-pilot theme.
- **Trust Badge Row:** Logos or badges for industries served (“Private Equity | Asset Management | Corporate Strategy”).

### 2. Pain-to-Promise Section
- **Layout:** Split column with copy on left, supporting imagery on right (e.g., before/after timeline).
- **Copy points:**
  - “Manual checks across spreadsheets, terminals, and decks slow your deal clock.”
  - “Fundley orchestrates a multi-agent AI team so you can ask, iterate, and decide in minutes.”
- **Micro CTA:** `See How Teams Use Fundley` linking to scenarios section.

### 3. Multi-Agent Advantage
- **Format:** Horizontal cards, each describing an AI role (Financial Strategist, Data Analyst, Research Scout, Narrative Lead, Automation Engineer).
- **Include:** Role icon, short description, signature contribution (e.g., “Summons structured metrics and highlights performance drivers in plain English.”).
- **Supporting copy:** Emphasize that the orchestrator keeps users informed (“You’ll see every step, status, and takeaway—no black-box guesses.”).

### 4. Product Walkthrough (Tabbed or Scroll Narrative)
Break into four anchored subsections with device mockups or screen captures:
1. **Conversational Cockpit:** Chat timeline, progress updates, flexible panels.
2. **Insight Canvas:** Interactive tables, metric templates, export controls.
3. **Research & Storytelling:** Summaries, chart rendering, meeting-ready narratives.
4. **Collaboration & Governance:** Shared links, permissions, audit history.
- Each subsection should include a 2–3 sentence explanation and a bullet list of user outcomes (e.g., “Review findings live in investment committee meetings.”).
- Provide an inline CTA after the fourth block: `Download the Product Brief` (ghost button for top-of-funnel leads).

### 5. Outcomes Carousel (Quantified Value)
- **Carousel or three-up statistics:** examples such as “10× faster diligence cycles,” “100% transparency over AI workflow,” “Live answers in client sessions.”
- Pair each stat with a short supportive sentence and small iconography.

### 6. Differentiators vs. Generic AI
- **Headline:** “Why Fundley outperforms one-size-fits-all chatbots.”
- **Comparison table:** Columns for “Traditional AI Chat” vs. “Fundley.” Rows for Specialization, Output Quality, Process Transparency, Enterprise Readiness, Real-time Collaboration.
- Wrap with a testimonial-style quote from an imagined persona (e.g., “Fundley finally connects data, narrative, and stakeholder-ready delivery.”).

### 7. Use Cases & Journeys
- Present three cards with imagery and layered copy:
  1. **Accelerated Diligence** – quick insight loops, peer benchmarking, risk flags.
  2. **Portfolio Doctor** – continuous monitoring, alert-driven follow-up questions.
  3. **Client Co-Creation** – live workshops, exportable deliverables.
- Each card: headline, two bullet benefits, mini CTA `View Workflow`.

### 8. Social Proof & Credibility
- **Options:** Logo strip, press mentions, analyst quotes, or short case-study highlights (“Scaled from whiteboard to live deployment in 4 weeks.”).
- Include security/compliance reassurance text: “Built with enterprise-grade identity, permissions, and full audit trails.”

### 9. Pricing Preview (Optional)
- Hero card summarizing commercial engagement: “Start with a guided pilot” + highlight support for team onboarding, dedicated success partner, and tailored metrics library.

### 10. Final CTA & Contact
- **Headline:** “Ready to accelerate your investment workflow?”
- **Primary CTA:** `Schedule a Strategy Call` (filled button).
- **Secondary options:** `Contact Sales` and `Join the Waitlist` (ghost buttons).
- **Footer:** Contact emails, social links, company footer copy, legal links.

## Visual Direction
### Color Palette
| Tone | Hex | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep Navy | `#050B1A` | Primary background blocks, header/footer, modal overlays.
| Midnight Slate | `#101B2F` | Gradient anchor, card backgrounds.
| Cobalt Accent | `#3A8BFF` | Primary CTA, key icons, timeline highlights.
| Electric Teal | `#4FE0C1` | Secondary CTA, progress indicators, subtle gradients.
| Warm Light | `#F5F7FB` | Section backgrounds, dividers, table headers.
| Soft Sand | `#F2E9DA` | Highlight strips, testimonial cards.
| Neutral Gray | `#7F8CA5` | Body text, captions, secondary buttons.

- **Gradient suggestion:** Use a Cobalt (`#3A8BFF`) to Electric Teal (`#4FE0C1`) diagonal gradient over Midnight Slate for hero backgrounds.
- **Accent glow:** Apply subtle radial glow behind device mockups using Electric Teal at 40% opacity.

### Typography
- **Headline font:** `Inter Tight` or `Space Grotesk`, bold weights (600–700).
- **Body font:** `Inter` or `IBM Plex Sans`, weight 400.
- **Supporting mono:** `JetBrains Mono` for data callouts or stats.
- **Hierarchy guidance:** H1 56–64px, H2 32px, body 18–20px for readability on large screens.

### Imagery & Iconography
- Blend real UI screenshots with abstract patterns inspired by market graphs or neural network flows.
- Use outlined icons for agent roles; consistent stroke width (1.5px) and Cobalt accent color.
- Incorporate soft shadows and layered cards to reinforce depth without overwhelming.

### Interaction & Motion
- Hero CTA hover: slight scale (1.02) with drop shadow.
- Progress timeline tiles: fade-in + slide-up when scrolled into view (~250ms).
- Carousel transitions: 500ms auto-scroll with manual controls.
- Keep motion purposeful; avoid distracting looping animations.

## Copy Voice & Tone
- Confident, concise, and pragmatic. Use action verbs (“activate,” “surface,” “deliver”) and emphasize tangible outcomes.
- Avoid jargon that assumes deep technical literacy; explain agent collaboration in business terms.
- Highlight user empowerment and transparency over AI novelty.

## Asset Checklist
- High-resolution product mockups (hero, walkthrough, mobile viewport).
- Role illustration set (5 agents + orchestrator).
- Testimonial quotes or scripted placeholders.
- CTA button components (primary, secondary, ghost).
- Icon set for metrics, security, collaboration, export, governance.

## Tracking & Conversion Recommendations
- Primary conversion: Demo booking form (embed Calendly or custom form above the fold and in footer).
- Secondary conversions: PDF download, newsletter signup, case-study request.
- Suggested metrics: scroll depth, CTA click-through, hero video view, comparison table interaction.
