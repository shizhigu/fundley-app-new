**Programmatic Calculations**:
All math is performed by our analysis engine, eliminating manual entry errors. When we calculate "Revenue CAGR", it's computed from actual data, not typed by hand.

**Source Documentation**:
Every number includes:

- Data source identifier
- Calculation methodology
- Time period covered
- Any assumptions made

**Audit Trail**:
You can trace back from final output to raw data source, seeing every step in between.

**Data Quality Checks**:

- Cross-validation against multiple sources when available
- Anomaly detection for outlier values
- Consistency checks across time periods

**User Verification**:
We show you the numbers AND the calculations, so you can verify our work against your own sources.
